Meet the Founder

Chris Odhiambo

Founder & CEO, ZetuBiz

Chris started out building websites under the Chris Tech name, working directly with clients to bring their businesses online. As the range of work grew beyond websites into applications, hosting and business systems, the venture evolved into ZetuBiz — a registered company built to carry that same hands-on approach to a wider set of digital services.

Today, Chris leads ZetuBiz with a continued focus on helping businesses use technology effectively — building solutions that are practical, well-supported, and suited to how each business actually operates.
