About Kadem Technologies

Technology, built around your business

Kadem Technologies is a professional technology services company providing digital services and solutions for businesses.

Meet the Founder

Chris Odhiambo

Founder & CEO, Kadem Technologies

Chris started out building websites under the Chris Tech name, working directly with clients to bring their businesses online. That direct, hands-on way of working — understanding a business first, then building around it — carried through as the work expanded.

As the range of services grew beyond websites into applications, hosting and business systems, the venture evolved into Kadem Technologies, the registered company that now carries that same approach to a wider set of digital services.

Chris continues to lead Kadem Technologies with a focus on helping businesses use technology effectively — solutions that are practical, well-supported, and suited to how each business actually operates.
